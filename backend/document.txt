ACME CORPORATION - INTERNAL COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT

Document Version: 3.2
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Classification: Internal Use Only

------------------------------------------------------------
1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

ACME Corporation is a global enterprise specializing in cloud infrastructure, AI-powered software solutions, and enterprise security systems. The company operates across North America, Europe, and Asia with over 12,000 employees.

ACME Corporation focuses on delivering scalable and secure digital solutions for enterprise clients in finance, healthcare, and logistics sectors.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. CODE OF CONDUCT
------------------------------------------------------------

All employees must maintain professional behavior at all times.

2.1 Respectful Workplace
Employees must treat colleagues with respect regardless of gender, nationality, religion, or background.

2.2 Harassment Policy
ACME Corporation has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying in any form.

2.3 Ethical Behavior
Employees must avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any situation that may compromise company integrity.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. DATA SECURITY POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Data Classification
All company data must be classified into one of the following:
- Public
- Internal
- Confidential
- Restricted

3.2 Access Control
Access to systems is enforced using Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

3.3 Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

3.4 Incident Reporting
Any security incident must be reported to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 30 minutes.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. ENGINEERING GUIDELINES
------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Code Quality
All code must follow ACME engineering standards and pass peer review before merging.

4.2 CI/CD Pipeline
All deployments must go through automated CI/CD pipelines and require approval before production release.

4.3 Testing Requirements
Unit tests are mandatory for all new features. Minimum coverage threshold is 80%.

4.4 API Design
All APIs must follow RESTful standards and include proper versioning.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Version Control
All projects must use Git as the version control system.

5.2 Branch Strategy
Developers must follow the GitFlow branching model.

5.3 Commit Messages
Commit messages must be clear and descriptive.

Example:
"Fix authentication bug in login module"

------------------------------------------------------------
6. HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

6.2 Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to:
- 20 paid annual leave days
- 10 sick leave days
- 5 personal leave days

6.3 Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

6.4 Performance Reviews
Performance evaluations are conducted twice a year.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. IT POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 Device Usage
Company devices must be used for work purposes only.

7.2 Software Installation
Only approved software may be installed on company systems.

7.3 Data Backup
All critical systems are backed up daily.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. COMPLIANCE POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 Legal Compliance
ACME Corporation complies with all applicable local and international laws.

8.2 Audit Requirements
Internal audits are conducted quarterly.

8.3 Data Privacy
All user data must comply with GDPR and other relevant privacy regulations.

------------------------------------------------------------
9. INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

9.1 Security Incidents
All incidents must be categorized based on severity:
- Low
- Medium
- High
- Critical

9.2 Response Time
Critical incidents must be addressed immediately within 1 hour.

9.3 Communication
Only authorized personnel may communicate incident details externally.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. AI AND MACHINE LEARNING POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

10.1 Model Usage
AI models must be reviewed before deployment in production systems.

10.2 Data Usage
Training data must not include sensitive personal information.

10.3 Responsible AI
ACME Corporation ensures fairness, transparency, and accountability in all AI systems.

------------------------------------------------------------
11. TERMINATION POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

11.1 Grounds for Termination
Employment may be terminated due to:
- Policy violation
- Poor performance
- Misconduct

11.2 Exit Process
Employees must return all company property and complete exit documentation.
